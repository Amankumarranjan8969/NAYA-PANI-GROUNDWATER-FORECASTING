🚀 How to Start in One Click

Simply double-click START_NAYA_PANI.bat inside this folder!

▶ START_NAYA_PANI.bat

The launcher checks Python, creates the local virtual environment (.venv), installs dependencies, and opens http://localhost:8501 in your browser automatically.

🌊 USGS MODFLOW 6 Physics

Physics-informed finite-difference numerical solver for 3D groundwater flow across 5 stratigraphy layers (40×40 structured grid, 50 km regional domain). Download runnable USGS MODFLOW 6 package bundles.

🌐 Photorealistic 3D Twin

True 3D regional world with real Digital Elevation Model (DEM), xerophytic Thar desert vegetation, real 3D tree OBJ meshes, satellite texturing, dynamic weather simulation, and 3D solute transport plumes.

🏛️ CGWA Statutory Audit

Automated Central Ground Water Authority statutory assessment per GEC-2015 methodology. Computes Stage of Groundwater Extraction (SOGWE %), NOC abstraction compliance, and Managed Aquifer Recharge (MAR) ranking.

📡 Live DWLR IoT Telemetry

Real-time Digital Water Level Recorder telemetry stream with automated Edge QA/QC anomaly filtering (rate-of-change spike rejection, battery health cutoff, and physical bound verification).

🤖 RAG Knowledge Copilot

Context-aware AI assistant synchronized with live 3D Digital Twin simulation states, physical bounds, LOFO sensitivity rankings, and grounded scientific hydrogeological documentation.

📊 Multi-Model ML/DL Suite

Compare SVR, Random Forest, GBDT, CatBoost, XGBoost, LightGBM, Physics-Informed Neural Networks (PINN), Echo State Networks (ESN), SARIMA, and LSTM deep learning models.
