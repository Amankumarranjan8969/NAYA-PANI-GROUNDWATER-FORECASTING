# Digital Twin

The Naya Pani Digital Twin is an interactive, data-bound hydrogeological workspace synchronized with the current seasonal workbook, trained ML and Deep Learning models, and the RAG assistant. Its scene organization follows OpenUSD-inspired hierarchical prim conventions (`/World/RajasthanBasin/...`) with explicit data-provenance tracking.

## Choose the displayed state and asset

The Digital Twin controls allow selecting a **Season**, **District**, **Administrative Block**, **Monitoring Well Asset** (either Basin Aggregate or an individual village piezometer/well), **Observation Year** (with temporal playback controls `⏮`, `◀`, `▶`, `⏭`), **Camera Viewpoint** (`Isometric Cutaway`, `Subsurface Cross-Section`, `Top-Down Spatial Plan`, `Focus Selected Asset`), and **Operating State**:

- **Observed (`Verified Historical Observation`)** displays recorded measurements from the matching workbook rows for the selected season, year, district, block, and monitoring well asset. Groundwater depth (`DTWL (mbgl)`), rainfall (`mm`), evapotranspiration (`ET`, `mm`), soil moisture (`Avg_SM` or `SM_10cm`), `NDVI`, `NDWI`, `Water_Balance` (`Rainfall - ET`), and `GW_Stress` (`ET / (Rainfall + 1)`) come directly from verified pipeline columns.
- **Forecast (`Model-Generated Forecast`)** displays multi-year recursive projections generated by the season's best-performing model (`Scenario forecast DTWL (mbgl)`), preserving village-specific spatial offsets when an individual monitoring well asset is selected.
- **Simulated (`Coupled Simulation Output`)** executes the coupled hydrological-AI scenario simulator (`twin_engine.run_coupled_simulation`). Users can select predefined Rajasthan hydro-climatic scenarios (e.g., *Severe Monsoon Drought*, *High Monsoon Recharge / Cloudburst*, *Intensive Tube-Well Extraction*, *Managed Aquifer Recharge / Check-Dams*) or configure custom perturbations for rainfall (`%`), evapotranspiration (`%`), soil moisture (`%`), temperature (`°C`), `NDVI`, and tube-well pumping stress factor.

## Coupled simulation equations and multi-model inference

When a scenario is executed in **Simulated** mode, the Digital Twin does not merely shift visual sliders. It performs a three-stage calculation:

1. **Physical Feature Perturbation & Re-Engineering**: Perturbs `Rainfall`, `ET`, `SM_10cm`, `SM_40cm`, `SM_100cm`, `Tmax`, `Tmin`, and `NDVI` within physical bounds (`security_engine.INDICATOR_BOUNDS`), recomputes derived hydrological indicators (`Water_Balance = Rainfall - ET`, `GW_Stress = ET / (Rainfall + 1)`, `Avg_SM`, `SM_Gradient`, `Veg_Moisture`, `Veg_Health`, `Temp_Range`), and updates rolling/trend predictors (`Rainfall_roll3`, `Rainfall_roll5`, `ET_roll3`, `Avg_SM_roll3`).
2. **Empirical ML & Deep/PINN Model Inference**: Scales the perturbed feature matrix with the season's trained `StandardScaler` and evaluates both the headline ML winner (`best_model`) and the deep-learning/physics-informed winner (`deep_best_model`, such as PINN, ESN, SARIMA, or LSTM) with training bias correction.
3. **Vadose-Aquifer Mass-Balance Sensitivity**: Combines empirical model response (45% weight) with an unconfined aquifer mass-balance flux sensitivity term (55% weight) using specific yield $S_y = 0.08$ and effective seasonal infiltration coefficient $c_{\text{inf}} = 0.18$ for semi-arid alluvial and weathered hard-rock formations in Rajasthan, outputting a 95% confidence interval (`Lower_95_CI`, `Upper_95_CI`) and a 5-year sustained scenario trajectory.

## Spatial geometry, 3D layers, and honest provenance

When the workbook contains complete `LATITUDE` and `LONGITUDE` coordinates (and `Elevation`), the Digital Twin positions village monitoring wells at their true relative WGS84 coordinates, interpolates the surface topography and piezometric water-table surface using Inverse-Distance Weighting (IDW), and renders vertical 3D borehole columns colored by water-stress classification (`Safe` ≤ 10 mbgl, `Semi-Critical` 10–20 mbgl, `Critical` 20–35 mbgl, `Over-Exploited` > 35 mbgl). When coordinates are absent from a workbook, the Digital Twin explicitly labels the terrain as an `Illustrative / Conceptual Geometry` cutaway and never fabricates GIS coordinates.

## Selected Asset Inspector and RAG Copilot synchronization

Selecting any monitoring well asset updates `st.session_state["digital_twin_state"]` and the **Selected Asset Inspector**, showing its OpenUSD prim path, WGS84 coordinates, surface elevation, historical DTWL min/max/mean, long-term linear depletion/recovery slope (`mbgl/year`), active hydrological alerts, and historical time-series charts. Both the embedded **Digital Twin AI Copilot** and the main **AI Assistant** page read this synchronized state directly to provide evidence-based explanations with source citations and explicit provenance tags.
